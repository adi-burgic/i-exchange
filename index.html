FAQ

Questions, answered

What are tokens?

Tokens are i-Exchange's in-app value. 1 token is roughly 1 EUR. You use them to balance trades when items aren't worth the same, and to pay for shipping.

Can I cash out tokens?

No. Tokens can only be spent inside i-Exchange — on trades or shipping. That keeps the platform about exchanging, not selling.

Is it free to join?

Yes, joining is free. Verify your phone number and you'll also get 10 starter tokens to make your first trade easier.

How does shipping work?

If a listing offers shipping, each of you sends your own item with Pošta Slovenije, GLS, DPD or a Packeta parcel locker and adds the tracking number. Each person pays for their own parcel — in euros or tokens.

What if something goes wrong?

Tokens in an offer stay protected until both sides confirm delivery. If an item doesn't arrive or isn't as described, open a dispute and we'll step in. You can also report or block any user or listing.

Where does it work?

In Slovenia only, for now — anywhere in the country, with meetups or shipping.

When does it launch?

We're getting ready to launch in Slovenia soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the moment we go live.
